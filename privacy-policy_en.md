# Privacy Policy — The Last Migration

*Last updated: October 5, 2026*

The Last Migration ("the game") is developed by Titouan Deronzier.

## Data collection
The game **does not collect, transmit or share any personal data**.
It contains no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and requires no account and no internet connection.

## Data stored on your device
To work, the game only saves the following **on your device**:
- your settings (language, volume, text speed, vibrations);
- your flowchart progress.

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from **Settings > Reset progress**, or by uninstalling the game.

## Children
The game does not collect any data, including from children.

## Changes
If a future version changes these practices (for example by adding usage statistics), this page will be updated before that version is released.
